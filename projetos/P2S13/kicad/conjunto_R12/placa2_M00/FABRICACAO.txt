M00 / R12 - PRELIMINARY RF AND PROCESS REVIEW - NOT A PRODUCTION RELEASE

Quantity: three identical M00 PCBs per complete antenna.
Board outline: 50 x 88 mm; two copper layers.
Core: Rogers RO4350B, dielectric thickness 0.76 mm.
Copper: 35 um per external layer. Core plus copper nominal 0.83 mm,
before finish; do not substitute a 0.83 mm dielectric core.
Finish: ENIG. RF model: NiP 4.5 um and Au 0.0254 um nominal.
No solder mask and no silkscreen. Mask Gerbers define full openings.
Four plated vias, finished diameter 0.40 mm.
Two plated cable entry holes, finished diameter 1.10 mm.
No NPTH. Excellon metric 3:6 with retained zeros.
IMPORTANT: simulated barrel wall copper is 35 um on this M00 board.
The separate C08 board uses 18 um in its RF model. Do not assume the
same via-plating process on both boards without engineering review.
Confirm achievable stackup, barrel copper, finish and tolerances before
ordering. Do not change copper, antipads, drill diameters or outline.
No assembled components or stencil. Cable soldering is manual.
RF performance and manufacturing process release remain pending.
